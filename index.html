    Mechanism

    The fee follows the NYSE calendar.

    OWL is the common pair in every pool. Each tokenized stock is paired with OWL, so a swap between two stock tokens routes through two OWL pools. One Uniswap v4 hook is attached to every pool. On each swap it maps the current time to one of four states and applies that state's fee.

    State Hours (ET) Fee Market condition
    Asleep Mon–Fri 9:30–16:00 0.05% NYSE regular session. Primary-market liquidity is deepest; OWL competes on price.
    Stirring 4:00–9:30, 16:00–20:00 0.30% NYSE pre-market and after-hours. Thinner primary-market liquidity.
    Awake Overnight 20:00–4:00 0.60% No primary-market session. OWL pools set the price until pre-market.
    Hunting Weekends and NYSE holidays 1.00% No primary-market session for 56 hours or more.

    Routing volume

    Every swap between paired stock tokens passes through OWL pools and pays the fee of the current state.

    Dislocation volume

    While the NYSE is closed, tokenized prices drift from the last primary-market price. Closing that gap is swap volume, and it happens almost entirely while the fee is 0.30% or higher.

    Fee split · buybacks

    70% to LPs. 20% buys OWL. 10% to treasury.

    Buybacks execute only while the NYSE is closed.

    • 70%liquidity providers
    • 20%OWL buybacks

    Every swap fee is split the same way. The buyback share is spent on open-market purchases of OWL, and those purchases run only in the Stirring, Awake, and Hunting states. That puts every buy in the hours the fee is highest, and every buy is a visible onchain transaction.

    Glossary

    Glossary

    Nest

    An OWL liquidity pool. Each tokenized stock has one, paired with OWL.

    Parliament

    The liquidity providers in a nest. A group of owls is called a parliament.

    Hunt

    The buyback program. 20% of fees, spent on OWL only while the NYSE is closed.

    Trade tokenized stocks at any hour, at a fee that reflects the hour.

    Liquidity hub for tokenized equities · Robinhood Chain

    Risk

    Tokenized equities track prices during and outside market hours. Prices can move while the underlying market is closed, and liquidity is thinner then. Higher fees compensate liquidity providers for that risk. They do not remove it.
