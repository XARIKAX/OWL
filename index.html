Thesis

Stocks close at 4. OWL clocks in.

6.5hours a day
×5days a week
32.5/168hours anchored

Tokenized stocks trade 24/7. The underlying trades 6.5 hours a day, 5 days a week. That leaves most of the week with no offchain anchor: thin liquidity, wide spreads, real dislocation. Every hub token on Robinhood Chain charges the same flat fee through all of it.

OWL prices time. Cheap when the NYSE is open and liquidity is deep. Expensive when it closes and OWL pools become the only price discovery for AAPL, TSLA, SPY. LPs get paid for the risk they actually carry. Arbs pay for the edge they actually take.

The flat fee hub subsidizes the most dangerous hours of the week. OWL charges for them.
